FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our taxi services in Mauritius.

How much does a taxi from Mauritius airport cost?

Our airport transfer rates start from Rs 900 to Mahebourg, Rs 2,000 to Port Louis, and Rs 2,600 to Grand Baie, Flic en Flac, and Le Morne. Contact us for a personalised quote.

How do I book a taxi in Mauritius?

You can book instantly via WhatsApp at +230 59127295. Simply send us your pickup location, drop‑off location, number of passengers, luggage details, and travel date. We'll confirm availability immediately.

Is it safe to take a taxi in Mauritius?

Yes, absolutely. Our drivers are professional, licensed, and experienced. All vehicles are air‑conditioned, well‑maintained, and fully insured. We offer 24/7 roadside support for your peace of mind.

Do you offer island excursions in Mauritius?

Yes, we offer private island excursions to all major attractions including Flic en Flac, Chamarel Waterfall, Le Morne Brabant, Grand Baie, Ile aux Cerfs, and Port Louis. Half‑day tours start at Rs 3,000 and full‑day tours at Rs 6,000.
